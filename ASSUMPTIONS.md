# Assumptions and defaults

Every point the owner has not decided is answered here with the **safest default**. Each is one place in the code or one setting, so changing it is easy. The owner's questions are numbered at the top of `docs/PROGRESS.md`.

## The design in one paragraph

A voter list is one row of `societies`. It has one common link (`/v/<16 hex>`), made when the list is made. Voters sign in with e-mail + 6-digit code and enrol one or more homes as records (`voters` table). The Organizer and Hosts check each record (Approved or Not approved with a reason, then resubmitted). A **draft** publication is public and replaceable; a **frozen** publication is immutable, fingerprinted and signed, at a date-and-time cut-off. Every state change is one database batch that also writes the signed audit entry.

## Roles and limits

- **1 Organizer** (a unique index) and **up to 10 Hosts** (triggers). The Organizer role can be handed to a Host in one step; there is still exactly one.
- **The standing host `host@townhall-records.com`** is added to every list automatically, cannot be removed, and **does not count against the 10** (D1 in `docs/GAPS-AND-PLAN.md`: it is the platform's seat, not the Organizer's choice). Changeable with `PERMANENT_HOST_EMAIL`. If the Organizer's own e-mail ID is that address, the seat is skipped.
- A Host checks, corrects, imports, undoes and sets weights. Only the Organizer changes settings, marks the link as shared, publishes, freezes, reopens, manages the team, erases, orders, and downloads **full** (unmasked) lists. Hosts see names, mobiles and e-mail IDs masked until they open one record; each opening is written to the signed log (up to 300 an hour).
- Nobody decides their own record.
- One person can organise up to 10 lists.

## Voters and records

- **Capacities:** Owner, Co-owner, Tenant, Proxy (and Member for "member only" lists). **Proxy appears only where the voting rule allows it** (rules 3 and 4). A proxy gives the owner's name and **must attach a Proxy Nomination Form** before sending. The list can require one supporting document from everybody.
- **Documents:** up to 3 per record, 100 MB per list in total, PDF/JPG/PNG, 400 KB each, checked by content (not just the name). Kept once a record is approved. Every staff opening is logged. The page tells voters to cover any ID number; **no Aadhaar number is ever asked for.**
- A voter can hold up to 5 homes. The same e-mail ID cannot enrol the same flat twice.
- **Consent:** a tick is required before sending; the time is stored. A voter can withdraw a record (approved or not) while the list is open.
- **Editing an approved record** sends it back for a fresh check; **the earlier approved details stay on the list** until the new ones are approved. The flat cannot be changed once approved.
- **Uploaded people are "invited", not approved.** They sign in, check their details and confirm; only then are they checked by the team.
- **Staff-added people are approved at once** (the staff member is the checker) and must tick that the person has agreed; the person sees the record and the notice when they sign in. A provisional (draft) list is shown without anyone who has since withdrawn or been removed; a signed final list is never changed.
- Sign-in codes: six digits, valid 10 minutes, stored only as an HMAC hash; staff sessions 7 days (12 hours for the standing host), voter sessions 8 hours. SMS is not used (cost).
- Text such as flat numbers and reasons may not contain phone numbers or e-mail IDs (they could otherwise reach the signed log).

## Publishing

- **Draft:** needs at least one person on the list; never blocked by check errors; replaces the earlier draft; marked provisional on the public page.
- **Final (frozen):** needs an event name and type, the word FREEZE typed, the cut-off moment passed (or "close now"), no check errors, acknowledgement of warnings, and acknowledgement of records still waiting. It then makes the list immutable (database triggers), records the cut-off moment, and signs the fingerprint into the audit log.
- Times: stored as UTC milliseconds; shown and typed in the list's own time (India +05:30 by default). The cut-off is a **moment**, not just a date.
- **Public masks (owner's rule, 13 Nov):** flat number; name = first 2 letters of each word + `****`; role; may vote; mobile = `******` + only the last 4 digits; e-mail ID = `********` + only its last 4 characters. Nothing else is public. Reasons for sending back, documents and anything else are never public.
- **Reopen** (type REOPEN, with a reason) returns the list to open; earlier final lists stay exactly as they were.
- The public page shows the latest final list if the list is frozen, otherwise the newest draft, plus earlier final lists, each with its own PDF and proof (`?pub=<id>`).
- **Several people in one flat (owner's decision, 14 Nov 2026):** any number of people may register for the same flat; nothing is blocked or merged. **The Organizer decides, person by person, who is authorized to vote** (a Host sees the people of each flat, with names in the short form, but cannot change this). The system never insists on one vote per flat. A flat with a single person is authorized by default (the Organizer can say no); where several people share a flat, nobody is authorized until the Organizer says so, and Step 5 warns (it does not block) about such flats. The weight belongs to the flat (set per flat when votes are weighted) and each authorized person carries it. A flat counts as "may vote" when at least one of its authorized people may; the totals show both flats and people. The same e-mail ID can have only one record per flat.
- **The first role is shown as "Owner / 1st Name in Registration"** everywhere people read it, in English and Hindi. The stored value stays `Owner`, so earlier signed records and fingerprints do not change. The names of the five voting rules (for example "Owner only") are unchanged.
- State-specific wording (Maharashtra bars proxy, and so on) is **not built in**: the voting rule is the organizer's choice, with a plain-words reminder to follow the bye-laws.

## Data protection (DPDP Act 2023)

- **Retention (owner's decision, 13 Nov):** voter records are kept always. There is no default erase; the Organizer's erase button appears only if the owner sets `RETENTION_DAYS` (minimum 30) after legal advice. A voter asks the Organizer to correct a detail; every correction is signed into the log. A voter who withdraws their own record leaves the list, and that record (documents, details, history payloads, and its line in the stored draft list) is cleared after 30 days; a signed final list that already held it stays as signed (a deliberate exception, told to the voter in the notice; the Withdraw dialog does not repeat it, a small follow-up from audit round 16). A record the list team removes is kept like every other record. Permanent keeping should be reviewed by a lawyer under the DPDP Act 2023 (purpose limitation, erasure on request).
- **The 30-day clearing is not signed into each list's log (accepted, round 12 L4):** the voter chose to withdraw and was told, the clearing is automatic and carries no person's details, and the list's own history already shows the withdrawal. If the owner wants a counts-only entry per list, it is a small addition.
- **No free text or contact details in the signed log.** Actors are pseudonymous codes (the Organizer sees a table that names them); reasons appear only as a code, and some entries carry names or flat numbers. Free text is scanned for phone numbers and e-mail IDs.
- **Privacy:** hosts masked; the only full export is Organizer-only and logged; nothing personal in URLs; public pages send no cookies and no analytics.
- A plain note for Organizers is in the README. It is a reading, not legal advice. A lawyer should confirm it before launch.

## Other defaults

- **E-mail** goes through the Cloudflare Email Service from `townhall-records.com` (decided 14 Nov 2026; the older Resend route still works if only its key is set), capped by our own daily limit (default 80 a day), under five rules: (1) a brand-new Organizer must be on the owner's list (`ORGANIZERS`) unless `OPEN_SIGNUP=1`, and only listed Organizers can start lists (a list handed over to an unlisted person stays theirs to run, but they cannot start more); (2) one e-mail ID gets at most 4 codes an hour and 8 a day; (3) one list's newcomers get at most 25 codes a day (`MAIL_LIST_CAP`), and newcomers together at most two thirds of the voters' pool, so people the list team has vouched for (uploaded, added, approved or sent back by the team) can always sign in; a record someone makes for themselves counts as a newcomer until the team has acted on it; (4) two daily pools, voters (codes and send-back notes, notes at most a sixth; the cap minus the staff reserve) and staff (sign-in codes and invitations, `MAIL_STAFF_RESERVE`, default 20); (5) the optional free Cloudflare Turnstile check on both sign-in forms (on only when both `TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` and `TURNSTILE_SECRET` are set). **Turnstile must be switched on before the link goes public** (it is a go-live row in the smoke test): without it one script can use up a day's allowance. Every code request gets the same words and takes about the same time (the mail is sent after the answer, and a mail that cannot go is not reported), so nobody can learn who is on a list. Known limits: a stranger who knows an address can use up its 4 codes an hour and 8 a day (Turnstile slows this); standing-host sign-ins are not written to each list's trail (it is on every list), but all its actions are; the flat field allows up to two words, and a staff approver sees it before it can become public. Owner question 7 is the size of the allowance.

- **Known small limits (round 10):** the 30-day clearing runs when the product is next used (about one code request in 20), so a record may stay a little longer on a very quiet product; a send-back note written by the team stays in the history after its record is cleared (notes may not hold contact details); a person the team removed is brought back with "Add a person", not by upload; the public CSV and PDF also carry non-personal columns (weight, may attend, note), which is the owner's call (PROGRESS question 3).
- **Mail slots are claimed in one statement** (count and insert together), so a burst of simultaneous requests cannot overshoot the day's allowance.
- **Voter language switch:** see the Languages line below.
- **Limits:** a list holds up to 6000 records; one bulk action covers up to 60 records; CSV up to 3 MB; imports 20 an hour per list; public list pages 50 rows.
- **Price:** the price slot is off until `PRICE_INR` is set. Every payment follows the owner's Games pattern: this site asks the central payment service of IT SERVICES BY RAJAT AGARWAL (`https://itservices-rajat-agarwal.pages.dev`, one Razorpay ledger) to open the order (`POST /api/pay/order`, header `x-site-key` = `PAY_SERVICE_KEY`, site code `PAY_SITE`), the buyer pays in that service's payment frame (`/payframe/`), and this site asks it to verify (`POST /api/pay/verify`) before an order counts as paid. This site holds **no Razorpay key and never calls Razorpay**. Without `PAY_SERVICE_KEY` and `PAY_SITE` only test orders exist. **Not connected to anything live:** the site is not registered in the central ledger, the payment frame does not yet allow this site's address, and this site has no pay screen yet (owner's side, and only if a price is ever set).
- **Hosting:** Cloudflare Pages Functions and D1, free plan. Nothing deployed.
- **PDF font** is Latin-only; non-Latin names print as "?" in the PDF (screens and CSV are fine).
- **Languages:** the voter page has a language switch (English, Hindi); the Organizer and Host page is in English for now (adding it is one more string file, plus a native reader's review). Each person chooses for their own view, remembered in that browser. Dates keep their English form (day, short month, year) in Hindi for now.
- The old tables of migrations 0001 to 0004 stay in the database, unused, so migrations remain additive and a rollback is simple.

## Still open (honest list)

- **Free-plan CPU.** Timings on a laptop (Bun plus SQLite, so only a hint): with 6000 records the overview takes about 140 ms, the final freeze about 1.5 s, the public PDF about 180 ms. Cloudflare's free plan allows 10 ms of CPU per request. Lists of a few hundred are likely fine; a 6000-record list probably is not. The preview smoke test decides it, and the README names the fallbacks.
- **Time zones** are fixed offsets, so a list outside India that observes daylight saving would be an hour off in summer. India (+05:30) has none.
- **The public proof** is server-assisted: the signature and public key are published, so an outside party can verify them, but the fingerprint covers unmasked data and cannot be recomputed from the masked public list.
- **Documents** are stored inside the database (base64). Each voter is limited, and a whole list holds up to 100 MB of documents.
- **The standing host** can open any record on any list (each opening is logged and shown to the Organizer). A second sign-in step for that seat is an owner decision.

- **Not run on real Cloudflare D1 or Workers.** The same migrations and statements run on `bun:sqlite`. D1 specifics (`changes()` inside a batch, batch size limits, the 10 ms free-plan CPU limit) are unverified; the preview smoke test in `docs/SMOKE-TEST.md` exists for that.
- **Browser check:** the pages were driven by hand in a browser at phone and laptop width during the build; there is no automated browser suite.
- **No SMS sign-in** (paid). **No direct Razorpay, ever**; payments only through the central service (owner-gated). **Real delivery** (Cloudflare Email Service and Resend) was only exercised against a mocked `fetch` until the preview smoke test.
- **Objection window:** the draft list plus voters' right to correct their records until the cut-off serves as the objection period. A separate, named objection step is not built.
- **Returning Officer** is not a fourth role: the Organizer, acting with Hosts, plays it.
- The research behind these defaults is in `docs/RESEARCH-NOTES.md`; several state-law points there are marked unverified.
- **Accepted small points (round 15):** a Host's corrections of a record without opening it are logged and can be undone, but are not rate-limited; "Start another list" is shown to Hosts although the server only allows listed Organizers; a Host's duplicate-name warnings run on masked names and may differ slightly from the Organizer's. A Host's check of an uploaded sheet is noted in the log (counts only).
- **Default authorization follows the head-count of a flat (known and accepted).** A lone person is authorized by default; when a second person is approved, nobody is authorized until the Organizer chooses (Step 5 warns and lists the flat); if people are removed until one is left, that one is authorized by default again. Uploads mark a lone person explicitly. Only the Organizer's own choices are written to the signed trail.
- **Head-count variant (round 23, known and accepted).** If a flat has one person the Organizer authorized and one undecided person, and the authorized one is removed or withdraws, the undecided one becomes the lone person and is authorized by default; the flat then leaves the Step 5 table. The Organizer can always open it with "Look up any flat" and say no. A removed person's mark is dropped by the 30-day purge, as the person's whole record is.
- **Why someone may not vote (owner's requirement of 14 Nov 2026).** Whenever the Organizer or a Host marks a person "not allowed to vote" they must choose a reason from a short list (dues unpaid, tenant, co-owner or not the 1st name, not a member, proxy papers awaited) or write their own (3 to 120 characters, no phone numbers or e-mail IDs). There is no silent "no": the server refuses it, and an uploaded "No" without a reason is flagged for the Organizer (an error that blocks the freeze until it is fixed). The reason is shown to the Organizer, to the Hosts and to the person (in their own status); it is stored with who set it ("organizer" or "host"). The record history carries the reason and the person's own words; the signed trail, which can never be erased, carries only the reason's code, who and when, never the free text. A reason the Organizer gave can be changed only by the Organizer. The reasons that follow from the voting rule, from unpaid dues (when the society uses the dues check) and from "waiting for the Organizer to choose" are written in words too.
- **Small points of the reasons (round 24, known and accepted).** (1) One reason is kept per person, though two separate switches can make someone "not allowed" (the Organizer's authorization and "allowed to vote"); if the Organizer gives a reason for a person a Host already marked, the Organizer's reason replaces the Host's and from then on only the Organizer can change it. (2) A person whose record is still invited, draft or sent back does not yet see a status; they see it once the record is on the list. (3) A person the Organizer said no to cannot retype their own flat (a plain message tells them to ask the Organizer); people who carry only an authorization "Yes" can still correct their flat, which clears the mark (round 21). (5) A frozen list keeps, line by line, the reason as it was written (including the person's own words, when the Organizer chose to show them or in the Organizer's full register); like the person's name on that line it is removed only when the whole list is erased, because a frozen list cannot be changed. (4) The setting for what the public list says applies when the next draft or the final list is made; a draft already public stays as it was until it is published again (Step 1 says so).
- **What the public list shows of a reason (default chosen by the builder, the Organizer can change it in Step 1).** By default only reasons that follow from the person's role (tenant, co-owner or not the 1st name, not a member), from the voting rule, or "waiting for the Organizer" appear on the public list, always in the fixed wording (a person's own words are added only when the Organizer chooses "every reason"); unpaid dues, "proxy papers awaited" and anyone's own words stay private, because they say something private about a neighbour. The "May vote: No" itself is always public. The Organizer can choose "no reasons at all" or "every reason". The choice is applied when a draft or final list is made and is stored with each line, so a frozen list shows exactly what was chosen at the time. The reasons are not part of the signed fingerprint (they are explanations, not the list itself). The Organizer's full register export always carries the full reason.
- **Who may touch the Organizer's authorization.** Only the Organizer can authorize, take away, undo, upload marks, or move a person who carries a mark to another flat (a Host's attempt is refused with a plain message). A person who is removed and added again keeps the mark the Organizer gave for that flat; moving to another flat clears it (and the signed trail names this). Step 5 lists the flats that need a decision and has a look-up for any flat, so the Organizer can always say yes or no to anyone, including a lone person. An upload's "Authorized to vote" column and its lone-person default never override people already registered in that flat.
- **Small points of the reasons (round 26, known and accepted).** (1) A Host can still get a person out of a flat the Organizer marked by removing them there and adding them again elsewhere (the move guard works on the record, not on the person's identity); the Organizer sees both steps in the trail and the new record starts undecided. (2) The "phone number" check looks for ten or more digits in a row (any script, spaces or dashes between); a landline written with a short code can slip through. (3) In an upload, filler in the Reason column ("na", "-", "nil", "none", anything under three letters) counts as no reason, so a "No" next to it is flagged for the Organizer to settle before publishing. (4) The signed trail records an upload's reasons and an undo's cleared or restored reasons as codes and counts only, never the person's own words.
- **Small points (round 27, known and accepted).** (1) When someone is marked "not allowed" on screen, filler such as "n/a" is accepted as the person's own words (three characters are enough); it stays private and an upload treats the same filler as no reason. (2) A sheet's Reason text is matched by its first words, so "Dues paid" is read as dues; the reviewer sees the result in the preview before saving. (3) A missing reason blocks the final freeze (not a draft publish); a draft made before the Organizer tightens the public-reason setting stays as it was until the next draft. (4) `MAIL_DAILY_CAP` defaults to 80 when not set; it is set to 20 on the preview only.
- **Round 29 small points (payment path), accepted:** an open order of the other kind (a test order left before the key was set, or a real one after it is removed) blocks a new order with a clear message until the owner's side clears it by hand; a wrong site key is reported to the buyer like an outage but carries its own code `pay_service_config`.
- **Attendance public list (round 7, replaces the round 5 note):** the public session list of a list link shows the time, how, state and counts (present and total) to anyone who holds the link, and no names, e-mail IDs or flat numbers. The title and the place are free text typed by the Organizer, so for a Voter List link they are shown only to a voter who signed in with the e-mail code for that link and is on its list; a visitor who is signed out, signed in but not on the list, or signed in for another list sees neither. A list made from an uploaded sheet still shows its titles and places to anyone who holds its link; the owner has been asked whether that should follow the Voter List.
- **Attendance, one mark per e-mail ID (round 7, accepted):** an e-mail ID counts as one person. In an online session it can mark one person present in a session, even when it is on more than one flat, and a second try gets a plain message. The Organizer and co-organizers mark by flat and can still mark the other flats. A family that shares one e-mail ID has one self-mark per session and the team marks the rest.
- **Attendance, the co-organizer's view (round 7):** a co-organizer sees names and e-mail IDs partly hidden on the page and in the sheet (columns marked "(partly hidden)", file `attendance-sheet-limited.csv`) and finds a person by flat or member ID only, as in the Voter List. The page says which view it is.
