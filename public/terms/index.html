What this service does

Voter List helps a society or association build, check, publish and freeze its voter list for a meeting or an election. Townhall Records provides the tool. The society's Organizer decides who may vote.

What is collected

Flat or house number, full name, role, mobile number, e-mail ID and any supporting document that a voter or the list team adds.

Who can see it

  • The list team: the Organizer and the co-organizers of that society, and the Townhall Records co-organizer who helps societies check records.
  • The public list shows only the flat number, the role and a masked name, mobile number and e-mail ID.
  • A document can be opened only by the list team, and each opening is logged.

How the details are used and kept

The details are used only to build and check this society's voter list. They are kept with the list for as long as the society keeps its voter records. A signed fingerprint of each final list stays, so that nobody can change it later. Every correction is written to a signed trail.

Confirming a record

Pressing the confirm button on the voter page sends the record to the list team. The press, and the version of these terms, are written to the signed trail.

Withdrawing and corrections

A voter may ask the Organizer to see or correct the details, and may withdraw the record until the list is final. A withdrawn record is cleared after 30 days. A final list that was already signed stays as it was, so a later withdrawal takes effect from the next list.

Limits

The service is provided as it is. The society, not Townhall Records, is responsible for who it lets vote and for following its own bye-laws.
